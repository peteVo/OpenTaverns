# Wildways surface brief

**Retrospective context captured at finish.** This records the shipped surface and its constraints; it is not a concept roll or an approved image composition. It extends the existing OpenTaverns platform. Preserve the incumbent Fraunces display and DM Sans body pairing.

**Mode:** Operate.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
Make a competitive shared tile game feel like reading and shaping a living nature atlas.

### OWN-WORLD
The map is the world: Forest, Meadow and Highland tiles meet Trail and Stream routes. Keep habitat texture, route marks, player sigils and Survey Markers legible in both themes.

### STORY
The player scans the open market, chooses and rotates one tile, finds an edge placement, considers influence and projected control, then confirms one atomic turn.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Give the growing map and three public offers immediate priority. Keep turn status, legal placements, marker choice, network inspection and score context close; stack these surfaces on narrow screens.

### FORM
Use the shipped nature-atlas map, tile patterns, local light/dark palette, and English/German/Vietnamese game controls. Keep placement staged until confirmation. Shipping art is pure SVG; no provenance raster artwork is used, apart from actual captured screenshots documented in the repository. **seed not used: brief-pinned extension**

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
