# Werewolf visual QA

Hallmark critique: **Philosophy 5 / Hierarchy 4 / Execution 4 / Specificity 5 / Restraint 5 / Variety 5**.

## Direction

Warm limewash and olive for the village register by day; slate indigo and candle cream for private nights. Fraunces roman and DM Sans retain platform typography. Original SVG houses and character silhouettes replace commercial card artwork. Stable seats form a village circle, not a corporate status grid. Equal public house symbols cannot disclose hidden characters.

## Render → critique → refinement

1. First desktop/phone browser render covered role reveal, Thief, Cupid, Lovers, Seer, pack, Witch, Little Girl, dawn, election, discussion, vote ties, Hunter/Sheriff chains, deaths and all outcomes. Critique: private phone decisions arrived below a large village, repeated bot names impeded recognition, and selected night controls had weak contrast. Refinement moved open private decisions above the phone village, compressed the private header, added stable distinct fixture names and a full-name/status disclosure.
2. Second render included narrow 18-seat layouts. Critique: night selection inherited a later light-theme color rule; crowded inner seats collided with central copy. Refinement strengthened scoped night selection colors, reduced the inner circle radius and removed central fine print on crowded phones.
3. Third actual screenshots confirmed readable Witch selections and reachable phone lock controls. The 320px circle still had colliding presence labels. Final refinement shortens crowded house illustrations and keeps presence/death details in the accessible seat disclosure. Final scenario regression includes Simple Villager, Defender, Idiot pardon/death and Witch poison in addition to Classic states.

Desktop screenshots use 1440×960, phone screenshots 390×844; the crowded village also covers widths 320, 375, 414 and 768. Automated assertions check no document overflow or browser exceptions. Shared Rules preserve an unlocked private selection through opening, closing and reload. Touch action controls meet the platform 44px minimum; public map houses are read-only when no selection is requested. Keyboard focus, native role dialog, labelled targets, non-color state labels and reduced-motion behavior remain available.

The separate multiplayer browser test completes an 18-seat match using independent desktop/phone contexts and a spectator, then starts a fresh match. Automated play does not assess human discussion quality. Screenshots are evidence for visual hierarchy and layout, not proof of screen-reader compatibility on every device.
